How PartTime Job Works

Follow these steps to find work safely, hire reliable staff, and complete every shift correctly.

For Students
1
Browse & Filter Shifts

Open Browse Jobs and filter by area, work type, date, payment, and minimum pay. Read the duties, timing, worker limit, and payment details before applying.

2
1-Click Application

Register as a Student, choose your skills, complete your phone and preferred-area details, then open a job and submit your application with an optional note.

3
Unlock Exact Venue & Contact

Wait for the owner to review your name, phone, skills, and application note. You will receive an in-app notification when accepted, and the Accepted Shifts tab will show the exact venue and contact details.

4
Get Paid On-Spot

Arrive on time, follow the owner's instructions, and confirm attendance. After completing the shift, receive the agreed cash or UPI payment and confirm it in the app.

For Catering Owners
1
Post Catering Shift in 2 Minutes

Register as an Owner, complete your business profile, then create a job with the title, duties, work area, exact venue, date, hours, payment, worker count, skills, and contact details.

2
Review College Student Applicants

Open the applicants list for a job and verify each student's name, phone number, skills, rating, completed jobs, and application note before making a decision.

3
Accept Workers with Slot Limits

Accept suitable applicants until the required worker limit is reached. The platform updates hired slots automatically and closes the job when all slots are filled.

4
Mark Attendance & Complete Shift

Mark each accepted worker Present or Absent, complete the job after the shift, mark the payment as Paid, and wait for the student to confirm receipt.

Account Setup

Choose the correct role when registering:

  • Students select their available skills and preferred work area.
  • Owners add their catering business details and contact information.
  • Use Sign In to return to your dashboard at any time.
Safety & Privacy

Only the general work area is public. Exact venue and contact information is revealed to a student after the owner accepts the application. Meet at the listed venue, verify the job before starting, and never share your password.

Payments & Complaints

Owners mark payment as Paid after handing over the agreed amount. Students confirm the payment in Accepted Shifts. If payment, location, job details, or behaviour is a problem, use File Complaint and provide accurate evidence for admin review.

Trust & Security

Safety, Privacy & Dispute Protection

We build a trusted community where students work safely and organizers get punctual helpers.

Location & Contact Masking

Exact addresses and personal phone numbers remain concealed from general view. Only confirmed and accepted students can see full venue details.

Owner Verification Badge

Catering companies are reviewed and verified by platform administrators. Students receive safety notices before applying to unverified organizers.

Payment Dispute Redressal

If a student does not receive the promised on-spot amount, our 1-click dispute filing alerts administrators for rapid investigation and resolution.
